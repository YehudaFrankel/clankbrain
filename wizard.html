What to do next

1
Extract the zip into your project root. You should have a .claude/ folder next to your src/ or package.json.
2
Open Claude Code in your project directory. It will automatically read .claude/CLAUDE.md and all rules. cd your-project && claude
3
Start coding. Claude now knows your stack, conventions, and rules. Try: "review this file" or make a change and see the plan-before-edit workflow in action.
4
The system learns. After each session, lessons and decisions accumulate in .claude/memory/. Each session is smarter than the last.
